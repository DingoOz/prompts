# How to Become an AI-Native Company

Based on a post by Jacob Posel ([@jacob_posel](https://x.com/jacob_posel))

## The Playbook

### 1. Create a single MCP/API gateway

Let your team connect to your systems through one unified gateway rather than a patchwork of ad hoc integrations.

### 2. Create a company brain

Connect your static context ("Who are we", guidelines, product docs) and your hot context (meeting notes, emails, Slack, active projects) so AI has a living picture of the company, not just a snapshot.

### 3. Create a company harness

Instruct AI on how to interact with your company brain and systems — the operating rules, not just the raw access.

### 4. Onboard your team into the harness

Create a self-improving loop. Let it learn from their work as they use it.

### 5. Create a model-routing layer

Evaluate and distribute work to the right model, at the right time. Remove vendor risk by not locking the company to a single provider.

### 6. Build autonomous agents on top of your company harness

Once the gateway, brain, harness, and routing layer exist, agents can be built on top of that foundation rather than bolted onto individual tools.
